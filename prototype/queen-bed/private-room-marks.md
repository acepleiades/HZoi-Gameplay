## Mark a private room for the current game

Take control of a Zoi and stand inside an enclosed room at their household's
residence. Click a walkable spot on the floor in that room and choose
**Toggle HZoi private room**. This makes an unassigned room with a sofa or
enough space to stand eligible for NPC invitations. Choose the same action in
that room again to clear the mark. Marks disappear when you leave the current
loaded game.

Both Zois still need a clear path with permission to enter. The room still
needs to be empty, with suitable furniture seats or sufficient floor space.
HZoi checks the room again before starting the scene.
